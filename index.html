FAQ

Questions, answered

What is Prescient+?

A premium Discord-based trading community for options and futures: trade ideas, live calls, real-time commentary, and an engineered view of the market through dealer positioning, gamma, volatility, and liquidity.

What’s the difference between Legacy and Vanguard?

Legacy ($125/mo) is full community access with trade ideas, live calls, and commentary. Vanguard ($225/mo) is a smaller, limited-seat membership with real-time options alerts and direct access to the team.

How does the 1-on-1 mentorship work?

Mentorship is a private program priced at $3,500. You learn the full process behind the trades (structure, timing, risk, and execution) with direct feedback tailored to you. Financing is available at checkout.

What is GEX Sniper?

GEX Sniper is an options flow analysis platform with gamma exposure heatmaps, AI-powered insights, and real-time market data visualization. Learn more.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Legacy and Vanguard are monthly subscriptions managed through Whop, and you can cancel at any time from your Whop account.
